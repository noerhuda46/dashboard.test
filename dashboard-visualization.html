💡 Rekomendasi Strategis

Pertanyaan 1: Bagaimana cara meningkatkan VALUE penjualan?

  • JAWABAN: Implement 4-Phase Growth Strategy dengan target +20% dalam 6 bulan
  • Fase 1 (30 hari): Leverage Rising Stars (Java Halu, Bunar) → +5% revenue
  • Fase 2 (60 hari): Optimize Stable Products (Parentas revitalization) → +7% cumulative
  • Fase 3 (90 hari): Monetize B2B Segment (Regional Blend) → +12% cumulative
  • Fase 4 (180 hari): Retail Upsell (Perorangan segment) → +20% cumulative
  • Financial Target: Rp 176.5M/bulan → Rp 212M/bulan (target 6 bulan)
  • Annual Projection: Rp 2.117M (2025) → Rp 2.542M (2026), +Rp 425M additional revenue

Pertanyaan 2: Apakah perlu menambah produk baru?

  • JAWABAN: TIDAK PERLU menambah produk baru
  • Alasan 1 - Portfolio Optimization: 6 produk sudah optimal coverage dari budget hingga premium
  • Alasan 2 - Opportunity Cost: ROI dari optimization (19%+) > ROI dari new product (2-5%)
  • Alasan 3 - Supply Chain: Established relationships dengan 6 origins = consistency & quality terjamin
  • INSTEAD: Develop VARIANTS dari existing products (natural, wash, honey processing)
  • Variant ROI: Expected +37% portfolio potential dengan investment Rp 77M
  • Recommended Variants:
    • Java Halu: Natural, Wash, Honey (3 variants)
    • Bunar: Natural, Wash, Honey (3 variants)
    • Parentas: Light/Medium/Dark Roasts (3 variants)
    • Others: Development as needed based on market response

Segment-Specific Actions

  • Big Cafe (56.4% revenue): Lock-in dengan Loyalty Program + Exclusive SKU development
  • Medium Cafe (40.5% revenue): Partnership programs, barista training, co-marketing
  • Perorangan (3% revenue, 68.8% transactions): Upsell to larger packs, E-commerce expansion
